# Combat 0.1.30: allies are back on screen, fainter ghosts

Only your clan is protected and hidden again. Allies outside it are always on screen and always take your hits, and ghosts are easier to see through.

- **Your clan**: Clan Protect, hitting through and the three hiding styles (disappear, ghost, gone, with footprints or a square) work as before.
- **Allies**: marked allies and allied clans are never protected nor hidden, and a hit through your clan lands on an ally behind it.
- **Ghost allies**: with the hide key, allies can still become ghosts, with or without the name above the head, and they keep taking hits.
- **Fainter ghosts**: with the new Stein Loader, every ghost is more transparent than before.
