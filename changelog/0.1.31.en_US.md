# Combat 0.1.31: item modules, Supply Tracker and a W-tap in the Autoclick

Combat now looks after your items: it says what is valuable, locks the drop, stores in the chest, highlights loot on the ground and shows how long your supplies last. The Autoclick W-taps on every hit, and Auto Protect closes the window to defend in an emergency.

- **Treasure List**: the list of what is valuable (item kind and enchantment from a level up). Item Lock, Auto Stash and Auto Armor use the same list; an armor piece on it comes off at the durability threshold instead of breaking.
- **Item Lock**: the drop key never drops a valuable item (or any item, with "All items").
- **Auto Stash**: with health low, or with its key, stores the valuables in the server's virtual chest.
- **Loot Radar**: highlights valuable items on the ground with a glow, a beacon or a marker, each item of the list with its own style, color and priority, with no item limit, an arrow at the edge of the screen and a sound when something valuable drops.
- **Supply Tracker**: on the HUD, how long your armor (the set or piece by piece) and weapons last at the fight's pace, and how much effect time your potions and apples give, counting whether you renew before it ends. It starts by itself in combat and only shows once something is worked out.
- **Autoclick with a W-tap**: each hit that lands stops and restarts your sprint by itself, so the next hit has the sprint knockback (needs Toggle Sprint). The timings are Normal and Tick Sync, and Tick Sync goes past 20 CPS. Precise is gone.
- **Auto Protect**: no longer blocks in menus; with health low in a fight, it closes the open window (inventory, chest, Esc or chat) and defends with the sword.
- **Enemies by tag** left "More options" and sits right below the ghost allies.
- Needs Stein Loader 0.1.45 or newer: open the Stein Client or the installer again.
