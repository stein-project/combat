# Combat 0.1.33: a refill that swaps worn armor, Auto Trash and blocking in menus again

Auto Refill's "Keep stocked" now just swaps worn armor for new armor from the chest, with no spare count to pick, and Auto Trash arrives to throw the junk away.

- **Block in menus is back**: Auto Protect blocks with the sword again with any window open (inventory, chest, chat, Esc). It had been removed by mistake in 0.1.31. No window is closed by itself anymore.
- **Apple and potions with a window open**: Smart Effect eats the apple and drinks the potions with the inventory, a chest, the chat or Esc open, and what it started goes on to the end.
- **Keep stocked with no number**: the worn piece Auto Armor takes off goes to the chest for a new one of its kind, by the piece's rule. If the chest has none, the worn one stays. When a piece is about to be missing it fetches one, and every chest trip leaves a spare of each piece.
- **Auto Armor always first**: the refill does not open the chest while Auto Armor is about to act, and closes it at once if a slot goes empty.
- **Use worn pieces from the chest when lacking** (option, off): with no good piece in the chest, it brings the worn one with the most durability left, so you are never without the piece.
- **Auto Trash**: throws away the items on your trash list, one stack at a time. Add the item in your hand or one by its id. It never throws a Treasure List item, the one in your hand or the hotbar (unless you ask).
