# Combat 0.1.34: Armor Gap, the Fight View and an honest target

Players missing armor now stand out, the clan options are reorganised around the Fight View, and the target element stops showing full health for someone dead or hidden by the server. Toggle sprint moved to its own mod, Sprint.

- **Armor Gap**: players missing armor pieces glow in a color (pulsing) and/or get their hit box outlined, seen through the players in front. Choose how many pieces must be missing, leave out anyone wearing a piece with Protection at a level you pick, the look, the color and the range.
- **Clan Protect in one choice**: Off, Block or Hit through. Hit through sends the hit to whoever is behind your clanmate in your aim (ally, enemy, neutral or mob): only your own clan is ever passed through.
- **Fight View** (the H key, formerly Hidden Allies): choose what it does to each group. Your clan can stay visible, become a ghost or be gone, and a gone clan's hits can go through or follow Clan Protect. Your allies can become ghosts, and enemies and neutrals wearing all their armor can become ghosts too, so only the players missing armor stay solid. Every ghost still takes every hit: allies are never protected.
- **Target: hidden health and wear**: when the server hides other players' health or the wear of their items (your own hits prove it), the target says so instead of showing fake numbers.
- **Target: death**: a target that dies shows "Dead" for a moment, also on servers that respawn at once or fake the death, instead of jumping back to full health.
- **Target: update age**: a small clock shows how long ago the server last changed the target's health and items.
- **Toggle sprint moved to Sprint**: the new Sprint mod (it comes with the Stein Client) brings over your key and whether it was on. Autoclick's W-tap uses it.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.35**: update with the installer before the mod.
